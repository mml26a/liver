# Main figure captions

## Figure 1 | Overall model development and geographic validation framework

The schematic is read from left to right. The first module summarises the GBD 2023 substrate: 204 countries observed annually from 1990 to 2023, with BMI-attributable liver-cancer DALY and death ASRs as outcomes. The second module shows the physical isolation of 163 development countries from 41 geographic holdouts and the sealing of 2019-2023 target labels before feature engineering. The third module depicts the three history-only candidate representations (transparent trends, tabular histories and ten-year sequences) evaluated in three expanding temporal folds with nested stopping. The fourth module shows the immutable six-task lock frozen by SHA-256 under the prespecified RMSLE and 1% simplicity rule: GRUs at one year and Extra Trees at three and five years for both outcomes, with no post-test reselection. The final module gives the two prespecified sealed-test takeaways: none of the 12 task-partition comparisons beat the development-selected pooled ridge, and all 24 task-level 90% or 95% interval estimates were below nominal coverage. Full estimates and uncertainty analyses are reported in Figures 5-9. RMSLE, root mean squared logarithmic error; ASR, age-standardised rate; DALY, disability-adjusted life year; GRU, gated recurrent unit. The source outcome is aggregate liver cancer and is not restricted to histologically confirmed hepatocellular carcinoma.

## Figure 7 | Tree-model explanation and prespecified feature-family ablation

**a,** Share of mean absolute SHAP magnitude assigned to five feature families for the prespecified tree model associated with each task. **b,c,** Twelve highest-ranked features for the five-year DALY and death Extra Trees models; the three leading features are highlighted by outcome colour. **d,** Distribution of feature-level SHAP values for the eight leading five-year DALY features, with colour indicating the within-feature standardised value. **e,** Change in seen-country five-year RMSLE after removal of each prespecified feature family from the task-specific tree model; positive values indicate worse performance than the full tree. **f,** Corresponding change in unseen-country RMSLE across all tasks.
