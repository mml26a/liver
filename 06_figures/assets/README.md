## Name harmonisation

Map joins use explicit display-name aliases only; analytical country identifiers and values are not modified. Examples include `Iran (Islamic Republic of)` to `Iran`, `Viet Nam` to `Vietnam`, `Türkiye` to `Turkey`, and `Sao Tome and Principe` to `São Tomé and Principe`. Countries without a plotted sealed-test value remain neutral grey. The two maps display the 41 countries excluded from fitting; boundaries are contextual and do not imply a position on legal status or sovereignty.
